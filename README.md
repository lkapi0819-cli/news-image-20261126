## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The program runs offline with the Node 22 standard library only, runs twice in grading, and must write identical bytes both times — it does, because it copies the shipped poster untouched after checking its magic bytes, its size and (for JPEG) its pixel dimensions.

Stories are drawn from Thursday 26 November 2026 reporting, cross-checked against the day's wires and same-day reporting: Reuters, UKMTO and SAFETY4SEA on the Hormuz mine claims and the separate tanker strike off Qatar, Reuters on the Kuwait drone attack and the Jazan refinery strike, Yonhap and MoneyToday on the Bank of Korea hike, its dot plot and the KOSPI close, AP and MarketWatch on the European and Asian closes, FlightAware on the Thanksgiving storm's flight toll, IDF and QNA reporting on the Lebanon strikes, AP and Reuters on the ECB chief pick, and UN and Emergency Lawyers reporting on the Sudan strikes.

Verification, before this edition was submitted: the layout script was run twice and produced byte-identical posters both times; `main.ts` was executed against a scratch output directory and its output compared byte-for-byte with the shipped `entry.png`; the poster was also read back visually, card by card, to confirm every headline fits its column and no micro brief overflows its two lines (the Bank of Korea card's final line, the storm card's flight totals and the ticker were each checked at full resolution); and every story on the page was checked against a same-day (26 November) report, with Wednesday's stories from the previous edition — the Bangladesh wage decision, the Samara refinery strikes, the Shenzhou-24 docking, the al-Shati strike, Dell's record quarter and the Cairo tanker fire — deliberately excluded rather than carried over, except where a fresh Thursday angle exists (Hormuz, via the fifth-ship claim and the UKMTO report; Sudan, via the Roseires dam strike and the curfew).
